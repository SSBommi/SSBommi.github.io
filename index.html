MonARCH 12U CubeSat — Deployer Interface Structural FEA

LASSI · Undergraduate Structures/Thermal Researcher · Sep 2025 – Present

Prior finite element models of the MonARCH deployer interface used boundary conditions that did not physically represent mechanical contact between the satellite and the deployer rails. Rebuilt the model in Siemens NX representing that contact through CBUSH spring elements, CGAP contact elements, and RBE3 multi-point constraints, with force diagrams characterizing rail-interface loads during launch. Updated stiffness values — 75 N/mm lateral, 100,000 N/mm axial — better match physical contact behavior and give a more realistic stress distribution under deployment loads.

Also underway: an industry partner challenge adapting MonARCH hardware into a cylindrical form factor, 8 in diameter by 1 m long, volumetrically equivalent to a 32U. The current 226.3 mm square cross-section cannot be inscribed within the cylinder's 203.2 mm inner diameter, so subsystem repackaging along the 1 m length is under investigation.
